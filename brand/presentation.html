What we heard

The brief

An open-source UI template that shows AI agents as characters moving through an isometric Three.js office. It plugs into any agent stack in VS Code or Claude, so the mark has to feel spatial, playful, and crafted without falling back on robot heads, brains, or sparkles.

spatialplayfulalivecraftedpluggable

Success: reads as characters + floors, avoids AI clichés, works at 16 px and in one colour, matches the app's coral and ink palette.

Final design

Floor Stack

An agent standing on stacked isometric floors: the characters and the levels they work on, in one mark.

  • Coral head = the agent; ink plates = the floors it moves between
  • Built on a 26-unit grid with true 30° isometric edges, the same projection as the app
  • A heavier small-size cut keeps the head and gap legible at 16 px
